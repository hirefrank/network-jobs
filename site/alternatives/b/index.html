Find jobs where you're already connected.

Network Jobs turns your LinkedIn graph into a private job corpus. Your agent discovers openings at companies you know, ranks them against your preferences, and drafts forwardable warm intros — all on your machine.

Install — two lines

$npm i -g @hirefrank/network-jobs@latest
$network-jobs setup --agent auto

Prefer zero-install? npx --yes @hirefrank/network-jobs@latest setup --agent auto · Then tell your agent: “set up network jobs from my resume.” Works with Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, OpenCode, Pi, Gemini & more.

Method

Five steps, one rule

review before ingest!

Résumé → profile network-jobs-setup

Import your résumé; the agent fills your profile and runs a preferences interview — location, remote/hybrid, IC vs manager, former employers.

LinkedIn ZIP → graph network-jobs-import

Free Connections export becomes a local company graph. Synthetic fixtures ship for trying it out.

Discover → triage careers-discover

Model-driven career-page discovery, JS-heavy boards included. Findings stage under triage/ — always.

Confirm → ingest jobs-ingest

You review, you say go. Only then do listings become normalized corpus shards.

Search → intro network-jobs + intro-email-generator

Prefs-aware ranking with scores, then a forwardable warm intro through a real mutual.

The rule: discover only stages — nothing enters your corpus until you explicitly say so.

Marginalia

Local-first, for real

underlined twice ✓✓
✓

No cloud account

Everything lives in ~/.network-jobs/ — connections, corpus, profile, prefs, résumé. No hosted API exists to sign up for.

✓

Your data stays home

Skills read local files. The only network calls are public career pages — the same ones you'd open in a browser.

✓

Model-agnostic

Bring any model. Stronger ones discover better; mid-tier is plenty for search and intros.

✓

Free & MIT

Open source and local-free. The only spend is your own agent's tokens.

Q & A

Asked in the margins

good questions!
+Does anything get uploaded anywhere?
No. Profile, résumé, and connections live in ~/.network-jobs/ and are never sent to a remote API.
+Which agents work?
Any agent that loads Agent Skills — Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, OpenCode, Pi, Gemini CLI, Hermes, and others. One setup --agent links all six skills into each.
+Do I need LinkedIn Premium?
No. Free export: Settings → Data privacy → Get a copy of your data → Connections only. Then network-jobs import ~/Downloads/Connections.zip.
+Which model should I use?
Yours. A stronger model for career-page discovery; mid-tier is usually enough for search and drafts.
+Does it message people for me?
Never. It drafts a forwardable intro naming a real mutual. You review it, you send it.
+What does it cost?
The suite is free and MIT-licensed. You pay only for your own agent's tokens.
